MAINTENANCE

Permanent delete all data

Permanently removes participant registration records and scan history from the shared Cloudflare D1 database. Staff accounts, passwords, and audit logs are not deleted.

Offline devices may still hold local copies until they reconnect. This action cannot restore data from the application.

Permanent delete all data

WARNING: Permanent deletion will remove the selected application data from the shared database. This action cannot be undone through the application.

  • Will be deleted: participant / student / employee / visitor registration records (including scores)
  • Will be deleted: QR scan event history
  • Preserved: staff user accounts and credentials
  • Preserved: audit logs (including a record of this wipe)
  • Preserved: app configuration and session secrets

Other devices that are offline will not clear automatically until they next connect successfully.
