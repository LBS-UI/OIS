WELCOME TO NEXTGEN

One platform for the whole classroom office

NextGen Smart Classroom brings participant intake, assessment scores, and QR identification into a single workspace — replacing scattered paper forms with one consistent, searchable record for every student, employee, client, and visitor.

Staff sign in once to register participants, record pre-test and post-test results, issue printable QR tokens, and export reports whenever the administration needs them.

01

Built for school workflows

Categories, fields, and scores mirror the existing offline process, so nothing is lost in the move to digital.

02

Private by default

QR codes carry only an opaque token. Participant details always require an authorized staff session.

03

Ready to scale

Designed to connect to a cloud database when configured, with export-ready CSV, Excel, and PDF reports.

HOW IT WORKS

Three simple steps

No technical knowledge required — for students, teachers, staff, and visitors alike.

  1. 1

    Select your registration category

    Choose Student, Employee / Client, or Visitor.

  2. 2

    Complete the registration form

    Staff record the participant’s details and any assessment scores.

  3. 3

    Receive the QR code

    A unique token is issued and can be printed for identification.
